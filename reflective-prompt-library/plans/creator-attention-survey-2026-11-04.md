## Transferable Method and Failure Modes

The useful distinction is **brand strategy versus content performance**: a high-engagement topic need not express a credible long-term position. Start with the audience, a real problem, relevant experience and a defensible point of view; use AI to help draft, not to invent the person's history.

| Method | Useful contribution | Failure to guard against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interview and source corpus | Reusable facts, examples, quotations and voice references | Treating model-inferred worldview as owner-approved fact, inventing personal experience or relabelling someone else's experience as one's own |
| Study successful posts | Candidate topics, formats and framing hypotheses | Selecting only the best 10–20 posts; account size, post age, format, paid distribution, news timing and platform effects confound the apparent pattern |
| Build a content map | Exposes topic/angle alternatives | Confusing combinatorial slots with distinct, useful ideas or validated demand; bulk generation adds selection and review work |
| Draft with human editing | Faster exploration while retaining editorial responsibility | Fluent or stylistically consistent prose passing as factual truth; a claimed percentage ceiling on AI quality is opinion, not an experimentally established limit |
| Offer a concrete sample | Makes evaluation easier than an abstract pitch | Treating a celebrity reply as conversion evidence or ignoring unpaid acquisition/revision effort |
| Measure accepted outcomes | Connects output to the original purpose | Using post count or views as substitutes for qualified inquiries, payment, renewal, understanding, trust or factual accuracy |

An outlier relative to an account's own baseline adds context compared with raw views, but it does not establish causality. Include ordinary and unsuccessful content where available and compare relevant platform, age, format and distribution conditions. Those controls improve interpretation; they do not themselves prove the method works.

### What the writing experiments support

S9 reports a preregistered online experiment with 453 college-educated professionals completing midlevel professional-writing tasks: ChatGPT access reduced average time by 40% and increased rated quality by 18%. That is task-bounded support, not verification of the essay's twelve-to-four-hour story, company profit or a 2026 product.

S10 reports improved short-story evaluations with AI ideas, especially for less-creative writers, while AI-assisted stories became more similar to one another. Better individual ratings and lower collective novelty can coexist. Neither abstract measures personal-brand distinctiveness, long-term audience trust or creator-business profitability; neither warrants a universal homogenization claim.

## Bounded Checks Actually Executed

| Check | Inputs and observed result | Limit |

| Growth and quoted workload arithmetic | `480/250 = 1.92`; `43.9/13.9 ≈ 3.158`; twelve-to-four hours is a 66.7% reduction | Does not verify definitions, forecast accuracy, representative savings or profit |

| Outreach counterexample | Hypothetical 300 independent attempts, each with constant close probability `1/300`: expected clients = 1, but `1 − (299/300)^300 ≈ 0.632735`; iterative multiplication agreed | The assumed probability is not an estimated real conversion rate. ## Falsifiability and Remaining Unknowns

- A prospectively defined client comparison including acquisition failures, full labor/costs, net margin and retained customers could support a narrower viability claim. It would not automatically establish a universal “most lucrative” ranking.
- Authorized product-boundary observations could replace the corresponding documentation-only qualification. They would not prove business efficacy; changed product documentation or platform contracts require fresh checks before operational use.
- A reproduced local failure of an existing TeaPrompt contract could reopen CA-SK1 for a minimal in-place repair, not automatically promote a new Skill or runtime.
- Unknowns remain actual profit and failure distributions, total workload, customer retention, data freshness/coverage/licensing, authenticated isolation and enforced approvals, publishing reliability, and long-term audience trust/distinctiveness.
- Declined to judge: author intent, overall product security, any real client's probability of success, and which future business will be most profitable. The inspected sources and arithmetic cannot settle them.

The documentation decision is sufficient because it rests on checked attribution, category corrections, bounded calculations and existing contract coverage, not unavailable commercial outcomes. These unknowns are explicit limits, not pending authorization to operate products or expand the task.
