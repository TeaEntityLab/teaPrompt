- **Decision:** three narrow in-place adoptions; no dependencies, separate metacognition prompt, new core/domain skill or runtime. Existing output headings, status vocabularies, route descriptions, primary-surface declarations and security/permission oracles stay unchanged.

## Falsifiability and Residual Risks

- A learner plan that guesses proficiency, advances on streak/fluency alone or ignores the supplied time/agency constraints falsifies HCC-1's fixture claim.
- A fixture that conflates better Brier score with discrimination/efficiency, calls missing provenance fabrication or claims source hashing proves efficacy falsifies HCC-3's fixture claim.
- A recommendation that treats a book-derived summary or an uninspected study as primary evidence, or applies a human mechanism directly to AI, falsifies HCC-2's fixture claim.
- The human value proposition remains untested. It would need learner-owned outcome measures such as delayed recall/application, task-specific confidence versus correctness, recurring omissions or selected-task completion, with a relevant comparison. Card compliance, streaks and fluent explanations alone do not establish benefit.
- Some useful supports are protocol recommendations without a checked quantitative effect here. Abstract-only access and the absence of raw-data verification remain explicit limits. New evidence can narrow, revise or retire guidance without changing the project's runtime or promotion boundary.
